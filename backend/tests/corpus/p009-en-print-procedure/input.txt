ROASTED
CARROT SOUP
BY A TEST KITCHEN • 3 YEARS AGO
A bright, simple soup for a cold evening. Every
spoonful tastes of carrots, nothing else.
YIELD: SERVES 4
Ingredients
2 tablespoons butter
1 onion, chopped
6 carrots, peeled and sliced
4 cups vegetable broth
1 teaspoon salt
Procedure
Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add
the onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
Add the carrots, broth and salt. Simmer until the
carrots are tender, about 20 minutes.
Blend until smooth and serve.
Kitchen Tips> A spoonful of yogurt on top is lovely.
