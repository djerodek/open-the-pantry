Buttermilk oat pancakes

Every Sunday we make these. My grandmother used to say oats make everything better, and she was right.

You'll need: oats, buttermilk and patience. Jump to recipe below.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Soak the oats in the buttermilk for 10 minutes.
  2. Whisk in the egg, maple syrup, baking powder and salt.
  3. Cook ladlefuls on a buttered pan over medium heat, 2 minutes a side.
