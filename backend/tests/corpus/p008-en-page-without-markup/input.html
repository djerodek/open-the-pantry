Bread pudding

Prep time 15 minutes

Serves 6

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cubed bread
  • 2 cups milk
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup raisins

Method

Soak the bread in the milk for 10 minutes.

Beat in the eggs, sugar and cinnamon, then fold in the raisins.

Bake in a buttered dish at 350°F for 45 minutes.
